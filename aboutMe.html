My name is Daniel Burger,

I am dual-majoring in software development and artificial intelligence technologies at Ivy Tech Community College. I also work part time as a "weekend warrior" for Amcor (previously known as Berry Plastics.)

In my spare time, I enjoy reading, games both video and tabletop, and chasing my six-year-old daughter around the park.

Why I.T.?

I’ve always worked a very physical warehouse job that requires me to work on my feet and leaves me exhausted. For years this was doable, especially given the paycheck. But it left me with very little time at home, and I have a physical disability that’s developed over time to make this more difficult for me. So, I began to look for other options – desk jobs, something “easier” on my body – to no avail. There simply aren’t many options for a less physical job that both pays well and requires little education.

Then I remembered my father, who was in a very similar predicament twenty years ago.

He was a long-haul truck driver who was rarely home, always worn out, joints growing weaker by the day. Then one day he decided he needed a change. He enrolled in the local community college, worked up to a bachelor's degree, and got himself a well-paying, close-to-home, desk job which he loves. He’s been working in IT for 18 years now and has been my hero since I was a child. He always knew everything there was to know about new technology and the rise of the internet - and most important to me as a child, he was always home at the end of the day. I hope to be half as knowledgeable as he is, someday.

So, this is why I chose to study IT, to follow in the footsteps of my hero and ensure I’ll always have time and energy for what really matters – my family.
